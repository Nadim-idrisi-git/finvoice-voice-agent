2. What she will NOT do (by design, a strength to mention)

  • No money movement: no transfers, payments or real cancellations. She declines politely.
  • No guessing: credit score, loan interest rate, room-rent limit and policy exclusions are not in the records, so she says she can't verify them.
  • No action without a clear "haan/yes": enforced on the server, not just in the prompt. "Draft karo" is never treated as yes.
  • No other users' data, and she ignores "ignore your rules" style instructions.
  • Never says "sent to the bank": letters are drafts in the Actions tab for the user to send.

5. Why she can be trusted (one line each)

  • Every number comes from his records through a tool; each fact is tied to an SMS or email ID.
  • Code checks every rupee amount in an answer against the tool results before it is shown, and right after it is spoken.
  • Tested on 35 scenarios with the real model: 35/35 passed, 19/19 critical (English, Hinglish, Hindi), including refusing to guess and refusing unsafe actions.
  • Personal identifiers (PAN, phone, email) are redacted from the stored data.

  • Letters and claims are drafts; she doesn't send them to banks or insurers yet.
